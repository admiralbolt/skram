Version 1.0.5
==============

* Right click to harvest now correctly applies farming fortune chance.
* Added napalm creepers to the nether.
* Added ender creepers to the end.
* Added dimensional scaling -- enemies in the Nether are stronger, and enemies in the End are much stronger.
* Reworked fortune. %chance scales past 100%, and amount of items given is based on total chance. 150% chance means you always get 2x items, with a chance at 3x items.
* Minor XP balance changes:
  * Slight buff to brewing, drinking or throwing potions grants brewing xp!
  * Slight nerf to digging (about -10% XP).
  * Slight nerf to farming (about -10% XP).
  * Slight buff to fishing (about +10% XP).
  * Slight buff to sailing (about +10% XP).
  * Medium buff to husbandry (about +20% XP).
  * Medium buff to stealth (about +25% XP).
* A handful of adjustments to spell casting times & mana costs. Slight reductions in casting time + mana cost for many spells.
* A few small reductions to perk item & level requirements (particularly for stealth, obsidian, and blaze rods).
* Fixed a bug where mana regeneration potions could be made from mana regeneration potions.
* Vertically condensed UI screens a bit.
* Added two new perks!
* Smelting down metal items now gives a reasonable amount of materials back. (1/3 of the total item cost).


Version 1.0.4
=============

* Fixed a bug where ranged advancements were granting significantly more health XP scaling than intended.
* Goat created by Decoy spell now has a proper name.
* Reduce volume of the sound played from the random lamp event.
* Tripled the XP gain from ancient lamps.
* Nether Quartz now in XP map for mining!
* Mega / Giga chests are now mineable with axes, and actually drop themselves.
* Font size of amounts for giga chests now decreases as the digits increase.
* Mega / Giga chests now have a dump inventory button to quick move all non-hot bar items.
* Buffed fishing xp again.
* Mercantile now properly works on Wandering Traders!
* Buffed stealth xp again.
* Mildly nerfed overall perk discovery chance (from X / 200,000 -> X / 250,000).
* Exclude Berserk from InfernalMobs config to avoid crashes.
* Slightly buffed botany xp.


Version 1.0.3
=============

* Fixed a bug where mana values were overwritten on spell swap.
* Fixed a bug where you received the benefits of the Genealogy perk without researching it.
* Items in GigaChest now stack past their normal allowed maximum.
* You now gain Mercantile on Piglin trade.
* Roughly doubled XP from ALL sources for ALL skills, except for athletics & acrobatics.
* Increased drop chance for spells from all sources.
* Blast furnaces & smokers now properly show ownership.
* Added cooked porkchops to cooking XP.
* Added Knockback Resistance enchantment.
* Actually exclude some particular enchantments from Infernal Mobs.
* Right clicking with horse armor no longer yells at you.
* Reduced level requirements for most spells.
* Ancient Lamps added! Every ~1 hour of time on the server all players gain a random XP lamp!
